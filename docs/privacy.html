PiDeck privacy policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

PiDeck is a desktop dashboard with an optional companion display. Google sign-in is optional.

Calendar information and purpose

When you connect Google, PiDeck uses your account email, calendar list, and event information to display your calendars and let you create, edit and delete events you have permission to manage. Event details include titles, dates, times, locations and descriptions.

Processing and storage

PiDeck connects directly from your PC to Google. Access tokens and fetched events are held in local app memory. The refresh token used to maintain the connection is saved on your PC protected by Windows DPAPI for your Windows account. Settings are stored in Windows AppData. Local events created without Google are stored in the dashboard browser profile.

PiDeck does not send Google Calendar data to a developer-operated server, sell that data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models. Calendar information is not automatically passed to the separate AI features.

Updates and companion displays

App update checks and downloads contact GitHub, which receives normal network request information. Downloads do not contain users' account tokens or settings. If you configure a companion display, dashboard content is streamed to that device. The optional built-in video link is unencrypted and intended for a trusted home network.

Disconnecting and deletion

Sign out in Calendar settings to revoke and remove PiDeck's stored Google refresh token and clear the Google event cache. You can also revoke access from your Google Account permissions page. Disconnecting PiDeck does not delete Google Calendar events. Local events can be deleted in PiDeck or removed by clearing its browser profile.

Questions

For support or privacy questions, use the PiDeck project issue tracker. Do not post passwords, tokens or private calendar information in a public issue.
